Good To Know

  • Performances in any language are welcome — Hindi, Marathi, English, all of it.
  • Basic backline provided: mic, PA, stand and a stool. Bring your own instrument.
  • Backing tracks are fine — carry them on a phone or pen drive and hand them to the engineer.
  • Keep it respectful. No hate speech, no targeted content, no explicit material.
  • First-timers strongly encouraged. Half the room is going up for the first time too.
  • Bringing friends along? They’re welcome as audience — no registration needed.
